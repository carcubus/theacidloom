El sendero que bifurca los jardines




What if a particle follows a trajectory based only on the interactions with other particles? On each interaction the particle has several choices and for deach choice there is a weight factor. Each possible trajectory from the beginning to the end of times have a value associated to it. The value is the product of the weight factors of all the interactions along the trajectory. The particle chooses the trajectory with the largest value of that life-long quantity. Time and space as quantities should therefore be accessory, since factors commute, even order should not be fully fundamental.

To make it graspable by my simple mind I am going to throw myself into some aggressive assumptions:

Let us assume that my mini-universe is made of many ½ spin particles with two possible states.

All these guys live their lives in this universe with a WELL-DEFINED SPIN ORIENTATION ALWAYS (respect to one arbitrary choice that could be any of the particles).

Well… you know what? Forget about all that I have said, let us better assume a universe with a single particle and many measuring devices*.
