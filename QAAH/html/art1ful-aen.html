20 minutes in the car on the way back from work can become a strange trance. Tiredness doesn't make me think less, on the contrary, thoughts pile up and parade on the background of the mind. Meanwhile, mechanically, my hands, eyes, body and whatever else makes up the rest of my being simply drive home. They already know the way and every turn of the road.

That's the time that conscience grabs the opportunity to badger me:

"You've been preparing this 'thing', this sort of quirky style 'blog'. Yes, it's certainly been a hobby, you've done it in spare time, almost automatically, playing with html tags, divs and javascripts and without thinking too much about what it should have as content, as if it didn't mean anything. But in the end, between some things and others, you've spent time on it, and the idea doesn't leave your head, so it must be someway important to you. Now you're just about to leave it there? Just like that?"

And I really feel like I have 'a something' to tell, but now, suddenly seeming ready for a first post, the task becomes an insurmountable wall... and I urge myself:

"If you've set out to build a blog, it must be to write something in it or are you going to add it to your endless list of unfinished projects?"

It's not due to a lack of ideas, those are there pressing, it's because somehow I don't see myself living up to them. Fears and insecurities conspire to install more and more doubts in me, and it is so that, trapped myself inside this delusional loop, I somehow glimpse what the way out should be, the subject of the first post.... yes, maybe I do know the solution, but I just resist facing it.

"Yes! you do know, just a matter of determination, but you're afraid to do the required exercise of sincerity, aren't you? You'd rather throw it into the shadows and remain with the illusion of what it could have been but wasn't than facing the fear of feeling the golden glow of that projected reverie fade in your hands leaving only a bit of dirty charcoal? All right, same old shit..."


Amidst all this mental jumble I've already parked the car and am walking into the house. I'm coming back very late. I know I should get down to writing, but, once again, I don't want to face that self-imposed obligation that I don't know where to grab hold of -I stop the annoying inner mumbling before it starts attacking again-: even if I know, I don't feel like it, simple as that. Tonight I cut and close with my conscience. I want to relax, escape, let myself flow in something inconsequential and then, just go to sleep. I'm saturated, I'll do it some other day.
